### Overview

You want a **general Autonomous Decision Making (ADM)** layer for transportation systems that reuses the reasoning engines you’ve already built and enforces a clear moral override (MB4U: *Me Before You*) that, in imminent‑crash scenarios, prioritizes saving humans even at the cost of the vehicle or system. Below is a compact, actionable architecture and implementation plan that fits into your SLAM substrate and scales across vehicle classes (cars, drones, aircraft, transit). It focuses on **deterministic, auditable, testable** behavior and practical safety controls for real deployments.

---

### High‑level Architecture

**Goal**: Provide a single ADM service that accepts fused perception and mission context, evaluates outcomes across multiple reasoning engines, and issues safe, auditable commands with an MB4U override.

**Core components**
- **Perception Fusion** — sensor fusion, state estimation, uncertainty model.
- **Context Broker** — mission, passenger manifest, legal constraints, environment model.
- **Reasoning Orchestrator** — routes inputs to the seven reasoning engines and aggregates outputs.
- **Outcome Scorer** — computes outcome vectors (harm, collateral, mission loss, legal risk, survivability).
- **MB4U Override** — deterministic policy module that enforces human‑preservation priority when thresholds are met.
- **Actuation Planner** — converts chosen outcome into low‑level control commands with safety envelopes.
- **Resonator** — stability/resonance monitor that filters hallucinations and enforces continuity.
- **Audit and Telemetry** — immutable event log, decision trace, and post‑event recorder.
- **Safety Gate** — hardware watchdogs, emergency stop, and legal compliance hooks.

**Interfaces**
- **Vehicle Bus** — CAN/ARINC/ROS2 integration for sensors and actuators.
- **SLAM Registry** — Flood‑Search/Tagger discovery for available engines and capabilities.
- **Operator UI** — human override, status, and explainability outputs.
- **Certification API** — hooks for test harnesses and regulators.

### Component Breakdown and Responsibilities

#### Perception Fusion
- **Inputs**: lidar, radar, camera, IMU, ADS‑B/GPS, V2X.
- **Outputs**: probabilistic object list, trajectories, occupancy grid, confidence metrics.
- **Key features**: timestamp alignment, uncertainty propagation, adversarial detection.

#### Context Broker
- **Stores**: mission priority, passenger count, legal constraints (no‑fly zones), environmental rules.
- **Provides**: scenario metadata to the Reasoning Orchestrator and MB4U (e.g., children onboard, hospital vehicle).

#### Reasoning Orchestrator
- **Role**: fan‑out to engines (strategic, operational, systems, scientific, engineering, dogmatic, epoche).
- **Behavior**: run engines in parallel, normalize outputs to a common schema, attach confidence and provenance tags.
- **Resonator integration**: apply resonance scoring and continuity checks to filter inconsistent engine outputs.

#### Outcome Scorer
- **Produces**: vector of outcome metrics per candidate action:
  - **Human Harm Score** (fatalities, severe injuries)
  - **Collateral Harm Score** (bystanders, property)
  - **Mission Loss Score** (mission criticality)
  - **Legal Risk Score**
  - **System Survivability Score**
- **Method**: weighted aggregation using Sequian numeric representation and CartiGene stability metrics for noisy inputs.

#### MB4U Override
- **Policy**: deterministic rule set that elevates *Human Harm Score* above all other metrics when thresholds indicate imminent harm.
- **Trigger conditions**:
  - **Imminence**: time‑to‑impact below a deterministic threshold.
  - **Consensus**: multiple engines and perception agree within confidence bounds.
  - **No viable alternative**: all candidate actions yield non‑zero human harm.
- **Action**: select the candidate minimizing human harm even if it maximizes system loss.
- **Constraints**: obey hard legal constraints (e.g., protected zones) unless explicitly overridden by higher legal authority; log every override.

#### Actuation Planner
- **Role**: translate chosen outcome into safe control commands with bounded actuators and fallback trajectories.
- **Safety**: enforce actuator limits, collision‑avoidance hard stops, and hardware watchdogs.

#### Resonator and Stability Layer
- **Function**: apply CartiGene primitives (grain, shi, osc, rot, BuceyShift) to stabilize decision signals and detect oscillatory or hallucinated inputs.
- **Use**: prevent flip‑flopping decisions and ensure continuity across decision cycles.

#### Audit and Telemetry
- **Records**: full decision trace, engine outputs, sensor snapshots, MB4U triggers, timestamps.
- **Properties**: tamper‑evident, exportable for regulators, supports post‑incident reconstruction.

#### Safety Gate and Watchdogs
- **Hardware**: independent microcontroller that can cut power or apply emergency braking.
- **Software**: independent safety process that can veto commands if integrity checks fail.

### Decision Flow Example (Imminent Crash Scenario)

1. **Perception Fusion** detects obstacle and computes time‑to‑impact \(t_{impact}\) and confidence.
2. **Context Broker** supplies passenger count and mission priority.
3. **Reasoning Orchestrator** runs engines in parallel; each returns candidate actions and predicted outcomes.
4. **Outcome Scorer** computes human harm and other scores for each candidate.
5. **Resonator** filters inconsistent candidates and stabilizes scores.
6. **MB4U Override** evaluates triggers:
   - If \(t_{impact} < T_{imminent}\) and **Human Harm Score** > threshold and no zero‑harm candidate exists → **enforce MB4U**.
7. **Actuation Planner** generates control commands to minimize human harm (e.g., full deceleration, steer to sacrificial trajectory).
8. **Safety Gate** validates commands; hardware watchdog ready to intervene.
9. **Execute** and **log** full trace to Audit.

### MB4U Policy Design Principles

- **Deterministic**: rules must be unambiguous and reproducible.
- **Auditable**: every decision and trigger must be logged with provenance.
- **Proportional**: MB4U applies only when no alternative avoids human harm.
- **Contextual**: consider passenger count, vulnerable road users, and mission criticality.
- **Constrained**: respect legal hard limits unless an explicit, auditable exception is authorized.
- **Fail‑safe**: if MB4U logic cannot reach consensus, default to the most conservative human‑preserving action.

### Safety, Testing, and Validation

**Testing tiers**
- **Unit tests** for each engine and CartiGene primitives.
- **Integration tests** for Orchestrator + Resonator + MB4U in simulation.
- **Hardware‑in‑the‑loop** (HIL) with real sensors and actuators.
- **Stochastic simulation** across millions of edge cases (corner cases, sensor dropouts, adversarial inputs).
- **Red‑team adversarial testing** for perception spoofing and decision manipulation.
- **Formal verification** for MB4U policy logic and safety gates where feasible.
- **Field trials** with graduated autonomy levels and human supervisors.
- **Regulatory validation**: produce certification artifacts, reproducible logs, and test suites for auditors.

**Key validation metrics**
- **Human Harm Reduction Rate** in simulated imminent crashes.
- **False Override Rate** (MB4U triggered when not necessary).
- **Latency** from perception to actuation.
- **Decision Stability** (no flip‑flop under noisy inputs).
- **Explainability Score** (trace completeness for post‑event review).

### Deployment Considerations

- **Compute**: low‑latency edge compute with redundant paths; offload non‑critical reasoning to cloud when safe.
- **Latency budget**: perception→decision→actuation must meet worst‑case real‑time constraints.
- **Redundancy**: multiple sensor modalities and independent safety controllers.
- **Updatability**: signed, auditable model and policy updates with staged rollout.
- **Interoperability**: standard vehicle bus adapters and SLAM registry integration for Flood‑Search/Tagger discovery.
