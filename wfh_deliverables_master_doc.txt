WFH_DELIVERABLES_MASTER_DOC_V1

Purpose
- Practical deliverable set for a WFH architecture/spec-first workflow.
- Conditioning is treated as post phase after architecture/spec MD completion and baseline AI build readiness.

Workstream Split
- Main workstream:
  1) Architecture review
  2) Spec sheet reconciliation
  3) Build/gate evidence capture
- Side workstream (optional):
  1) Research notes
  2) Dataset concepting/prep
  3) Personal external research notes (non-authoritative)

Core Deliverables (DOC-style)
1) Architecture Review Narrative
- File: wfh_architecture_review_template.doc.txt
- Goal: summarize current architecture state, authority boundaries, and unresolved risks.

2) Spec Reconciliation Narrative
- File: wfh_spec_reconciliation_template.doc.txt
- Goal: map what is implemented vs required by spec and define decisions.

3) Post-Phase Conditioning Plan
- File: wfh_post_phase_conditioning_plan.doc.txt
- Goal: define Process-1 sequence and post-phase entry criteria.

4) Weekly Executive Summary
- File: wfh_weekly_exec_summary_template.doc.txt
- Goal: simple non-technical summary for progress, risks, next actions.

Core Deliverables (XLS-style / sheet-ready CSV)
1) Deliverable Tracker
- File: wfh_deliverables_tracker.csv
- Tracks owner/status/due date/evidence.

2) Architecture-Spec Gap Register
- File: wfh_arch_spec_gap_register.csv
- Tracks each gap, severity, decision, and closure evidence.

3) Risk and Controls Register
- File: wfh_risk_register.csv
- Tracks risk probability/impact/mitigation/residual risk.

4) Research and Dataset Log
- File: wfh_research_dataset_log.csv
- Tracks optional side-work ideas, data source, readiness, policy notes.

5) Business and Financial Planning Sheet
- File: wfh_business_financial_planning_sheet.csv
- Tracks plan assumptions, runway model inputs, scenario notes.

Suggested Weekly Output Cadence
- Monday: architecture and spec delta capture
- Wednesday: gap/risk update and evidence links
- Friday: executive summary + next-week plan

Definition of Done (Per Week)
- At least one architecture narrative update
- At least one spec reconciliation update
- Gap register and risk register refreshed
- Executive summary published
